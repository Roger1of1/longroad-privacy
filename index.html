Longroad Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

The Short Version

Longroad has no servers and no accounts. Everything it calculates is calculated on this iPhone, and everything it stores stays on this iPhone.

The app sends nothing to us, the developer: not your health data, not how you use the app, nothing at all. There are no ads, no analytics services and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

Your data leaves this iPhone only when you take it out yourself, as an encrypted backup you export or a diagnostics report you choose to share.

Health Data Longroad Reads

With your permission, Longroad reads these from Apple Health: your date of birth, biological sex and wheelchair use; sleep; heart rate, resting heart rate, walking heart rate and heart rate recovery; steps, wheelchair pushes, stand time and walking speed; VO2 max estimates; workouts, with details such as effort level and weather; and the periods you log in Cycle Tracking.

If Apple Watch is your source for nightly data, it also reads heart rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, active energy and physical effort. If your profile in Health is female, it also reads the contraception, pregnancy and lactation you log in Cycle Tracking, only to understand your cycle. It also reads blood pressure readings from a cuff and Apple Watch blood pressure notifications, used only for your body age.

Longroad only reads from Apple Health and never writes to it. Health data is used only to draw your scene, energy forecast and trends on this iPhone, and never for advertising or marketing. You can turn off any of these permissions at any time in the Health app under Sharing, then Apps.

Where Nightly Data Comes From

Longroad gets its nightly data from Apple Watch, through Apple Health on this iPhone. It doesn't connect to any other device or online service to get your data.

The data it reads is the Apple Health list above, including the overnight readings Apple Watch records while you wear it to sleep.

Where Your Data Is Kept

Longroad keeps its data in its own storage on this iPhone, which only the app and its widgets can read. That storage is excluded from iCloud Backup, so it isn't copied to iCloud.

Notifications are created on this iPhone. Longroad doesn't use a push notification service.

Backups and Diagnostics

The only way to copy your data off this iPhone is the encrypted export in Settings. A backup is made only when you ask for one, is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and goes only where you send it. Longroad never exports anything on its own.

If you choose to share diagnostics from Settings, Longroad first shows you the full report. It covers how the app is running (versions, counts, error codes, and how much processor time and memory it used) and contains no health values, account IDs or tokens. It's shared only if you send it.

If you've turned on sharing with app developers in your iPhone's Analytics & Improvements settings, Apple may share crash reports with us under Apple's terms.

Purchases and Free Trial

Longroad is a one-time purchase. The 14-day free trial and the unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID; the app only receives Apple's signed record of which of the two products you obtained and when, and it checks that record on your iPhone.

When Access Ends

If the trial ends or a purchase is refunded, Longroad stops syncing new data and stops its daily analysis until you unlock it. The data already on your iPhone stays there until you delete it, and you can still export or delete it at any time.

Deleting Your Data

Delete All Data in Settings removes everything Longroad stored on your iPhone. It doesn't change data in Apple Health, and it doesn't undo your App Store purchase (you can restore it).

Deleting the app also deletes its data. If anything it kept in the iOS keychain remains after the app is deleted, the app removes it the next time it's installed.

Health Information

Longroad is a general wellness app. Its scene and suggestions help you notice patterns in your own sleep, activity and energy. They don't tell you whether you have any health condition.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app with a new date. If you have questions, contact us through the support link on Longroad's App Store page.

For general wellness only. Not a medical device. Talk with your doctor about medical decisions.

Longroad 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 23 日

简要说明

Longroad 没有服务器，也没有账户。所有计算都在这台 iPhone 上完成，存下的数据也只留在这台 iPhone 上。

App 不向我们（开发者）发送任何东西：没有健康数据，没有使用情况，什么都没有。没有广告、没有统计分析服务、没有跟踪，你的数据从不出售，也不分享给任何人。

只有你亲自操作，数据才会离开这台 iPhone：你导出的加密备份，或你选择分享的运行报告。

读取哪些健康数据

经你允许，Longroad 从 Apple 健康读取：生日、生理性别与轮椅使用；睡眠；心率、静息心率、步行心率与心率恢复；步数、轮椅推动次数、站立时间与步行速度；VO2 max 估计值；锻炼记录及其详情（如运动强度、天气）；以及你在「周期跟踪」里记的经期。

如果夜间数据来自 Apple Watch，还读取心率变异性、呼吸率、血氧、腕温、活动能量与体能消耗。如果健康 app 里的档案是女性，还读取你在「周期跟踪」里记的避孕、妊娠与哺乳，只用来理解你的周期。另外读取血压计测的血压与 Apple Watch 的血压提示，只用于身体年龄。

Longroad 只读取 Apple 健康，从不写入。健康数据只用来在这台 iPhone 上画出你的画面、精神预报与趋势，从不用于广告或营销。随时可以在健康 app「共享」里的 App 中关掉任何一项权限。

夜间数据从哪来

Longroad 的夜间数据来自 Apple Watch，经这台 iPhone 上的 Apple 健康读取。它不连接任何其他设备或在线服务来获取你的数据。

它读取的就是上面列出的 Apple 健康数据，包括你戴 Apple Watch 睡觉时手表记下的夜间读数。

数据存在哪里

Longroad 的数据存在这台 iPhone 上它自己的存储里，只有 app 和它的小组件能读。这份存储排除在 iCloud 备份之外，不会被复制到 iCloud。

通知在这台 iPhone 上生成，Longroad 不使用推送服务。

备份与运行报告

把数据带出这台 iPhone 的唯一途径，是设置里的加密导出。备份只在你要求时生成，用你设的口令加密，只去你发送的地方。Longroad 从不自行导出任何东西。

在设置里选择分享运行报告时，Longroad 会先给你看报告全文。报告只讲 app 的运行情况（版本、计数、错误码，以及用了多少处理器时间与内存），不含任何健康数值，也不含账户编号或令牌；只有你自己发送才会分享出去。

如果你在 iPhone 的「分析与改进」设置里打开了与 App 开发者共享，Apple 可能按它的条款把崩溃报告提供给我们。

购买与免费试用

Longroad 是一次性购买。14 天免费试用与解锁完全由 Apple 经 App Store 处理。我们看不到你的付款信息或 Apple ID；app 只收到 Apple 签名的记录，写着你得到了两件商品中的哪一件、在什么时候，并在你的 iPhone 上核对这份记录。

访问结束时

试用到期或购买被退款后，Longroad 停止同步新数据、停止每天的分析，直到你解锁。已经在 iPhone 上的数据留在那里，直到你删除；导出与删除随时都可以做。

删除你的数据

设置里的「删除全部数据」会删除 Longroad 存在你 iPhone 上的一切。它不改动 Apple 健康里的数据，也不撤销你在 App Store 的购买（可以恢复购买）。

删除 app 也会删除它的数据。如果删除 app 之后 iOS 钥匙串里还留着它存的东西，下次安装时 app 会把它们删掉。

健康信息

Longroad 是一般健康类 app。它的画面与建议帮你留意自己睡眠、活动与精神头的规律，不判断你是否有任何健康问题。

政策变更

这份政策如有变更，更新后的版本会带着新的日期出现在 app 里。有疑问的话，请通过 Longroad 在 App Store 页面上的支持链接联系我们。

仅供健康参考，非医疗器械；医疗决定请咨询医生。
